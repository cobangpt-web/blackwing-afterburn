# BLACKWING // AFTERBURN

## Experience

The player feels like an untouchable ace because the game constantly turns dangerous close calls into immediate offensive power.

## Profile

- Real-time, continuous 2D, solo play against authored and seeded procedural waves.
- A complete five-stage run lasts roughly five to eight minutes and ends in victory or defeat.
- Execution and pattern mastery are the primary engagement sources.
- Delivery targets desktop and mobile browsers plus standard gamepads.
- Player-visible language is isolated in `public/strings.js`.

## Player verbs

- Move to aim, evade, collect energy, and select missile targets.
- Auto-fire the cannon while combat is active.
- Launch a multi-lock missile volley after acquiring visible targets.
- Trigger overdrive for a brief shielded speed burst and stronger cannon fire.
- Pause, restart, mute, and reduce screen shake at any time.

## Combat arc

1. Tempest Sea: light formations lead into the Varka sky carrier.
2. Red Canyon: denser ground-fire pressure leads into the Moloch siege dreadnought.
3. Neon Blockade: fast lateral attacks lead into the Phantom stealth bomber.
4. Frozen Stratosphere: spiral projectile fields lead into the Boreas weather engine.
5. Black Sky: the full enemy roster leads into the Erebus orbital fortress.

After stages one through four, the player chooses one of three weighted combat cores. Cores persist through the run and stack to level three across weapon, subweapon, defense, and engine categories.

## Resources and feedback

- Hull is depleted by contact and enemy projectiles.
- Overdrive is earned by destruction, pickups, and near misses, then spent as a defensive/offensive comeback.
- Missile charges replenish over time and from gold pickups.
- Combo rises with uninterrupted kills and converts mastery into score.
- Every hit produces a flash, sound, score echo, and short configurable shake.

## Art contract

Polished arcade illustration with crisp cel-shaded gradients and subtle metallic texture. Aircraft use razor-sharp aerodynamic silhouettes, beveled panels, and restrained luminous edges. Environments sit in deep navy, storm teal, and charcoal; the hero jet is bright titanium with cyan energy accents, enemies use aggressive crimson-orange signals, and pickups glow electric gold. Cinematic high-altitude dusk lighting creates a tense, premium military-futurist mood. High contrast, clean readable silhouettes, and a consistent top-down perspective unify every asset.

## Reference play route

Start the sortie, clear the Tempest Sea, choose a combat core, carry that build through the next three sectors, destroy Erebus in Black Sky, and restart from the campaign result screen.
